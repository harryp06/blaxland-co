Privacy & terms

Privacy policy and terms of sale for BLAXLAND 30, an Australian online store.

What we collect and why
To fulfil your order we collect your name, email address and shipping address. We use those details to process and ship your order and to provide support. We never sell your data. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing us.
Services we use
Wix runs our store, checkout and payments. When you browse this site, Wix stores an anonymous session token in your browser's local storage so that your cart is remembered; it does not identify you. When you check out you are taken to a Wix-hosted payment page, where Wix and its payment provider process your payment and hold your order. We never see or store your card details. GitHub Pages hosts this website's files. The pages themselves set no cookies and run no analytics or advertising trackers.
