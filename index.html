Heights and sizes

Three heights. Five size bands.

Heights

0.6" (15mm), 1.0" (25mm) and 1.4" (35mm). 0.6" suits most shoes, including low-cut sneakers. 1.0" and 1.4" need more room, so they suit boots, high-tops and roomier leather shoes.

Sizes

Five bands in Australian men's sizes: 2.5–3.5, 4–5.5, 6–6.5, 7–8.5 and 9–10. Between sizes? Pick the larger one and trim to fit.
