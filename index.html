Questions

Before you order.

How much height will I gain?
Choose 0.6" (15mm), 1.0" (25mm) or 1.4" (35mm) of heel lift. That figure is the thickness of the insole, not a promise about your standing height — some of the lift is absorbed by your foot and the shoe, and how much varies between people and between shoes. Start with 0.6" for low-cut shoes and go taller in boots and high-tops.
Can people tell I'm wearing them?
Not if you match the lift to the shoe. The insole sits completely inside, so there's no visible heel.
Will they fit my shoes?
0.6" suits most shoes, including low-cut sneakers. 1.0" and 1.4" need more room, so they suit boots, high-tops and roomier leather shoes.
What are they made of?
EVA (ethylene-vinyl acetate), a flexible, lightweight foam. We chose it for comfort, shock absorption and, of course, the lift.
What sizes do they come in?
Five EU size bands: 35-36, 37-38, 39-40, 41-42 and 43-44 (roughly US Men's 3.5 to 10.5). Between sizes? Pick the larger band and trim to fit.
How long does shipping take?
We ship within Australia only. Shipping is calculated and shown at checkout, before you pay. We'll email you a tracking link when your order ships. We're currently confirming delivery times with our supplier and will publish them here rather than guess.
What if something's wrong with my order?
Email support@blaxland.co with your order number. If an item is faulty or not as described you are entitled to a repair, replacement or refund under the Australian Consumer Law, and nothing we say takes that away.
